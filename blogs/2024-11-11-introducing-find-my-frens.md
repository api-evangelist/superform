Find My Frens is a 2 week campaign where Superform Safari participants maximize their Superform XP by completing their SuperFrens NFT collection before the Season 1 snapshot. Superform will conduct a snapshot of the SuperFrens NFTs you hold in your wallet on Thursday, November 21st at 14:00 UTC. Your Season 1 XP boost will be determined by the SuperFrens in your wallet at the time of the snapshot.
